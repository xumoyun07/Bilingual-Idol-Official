## Palette Evidence and Direction

The official website visibly uses a saturated royal blue navigation bar and a blue/white logo. The redesigned public theme retains the current project’s deep ink base for readability and introduces a restrained royal-blue brand accent, softened blue-gray surfaces, white space and a warm sand highlight. This preserves recognition without copying the dense legacy visual treatment.

| Token | Value | Public purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Brand blue | `#173FAD` | Primary links, active states and focus rings. |
| Ink | `#10253E` | Headings, navigation text and high-contrast actions. |

## Content Rules Applied

Public pages use fact-led wording, clear next steps and price guidance that links to enquiry rather than an unsupported payment path. Course facts are not invented where the source has no confirmed detail. Conflicting contact details are isolated in this note until the owner selects a source of truth.
